How to play

How do two phones get into the same game?
One person opens the app and picks a category. The app shows a QR code and a short link. The other person scans the QR code or opens the link in their phone's browser. Both phones are now in the same session.
Does my date need to install the app?
No. The host plays in the iOS app. The other player joins from any phone's web browser using the QR code or the share link.
What is the difference between "after each" and "at the end"?
"After each" syncs you up: both answer, then you reveal the result together before moving on. "At the end" lets each person go at their own pace, and you both see every answer at the end.
How long is a round?
Twenty questions. Most rounds take ten to twenty minutes depending on how much you talk between answers.

Common issues

The other phone joined but the game is stuck.
Both phones need an internet connection. If one phone goes offline, pull it back online and the session usually resumes. If it does not, restart the round and try again.
The QR code does not scan.
Try the short share link instead. You can copy it from the same screen. Older phone cameras sometimes have trouble with QR codes in low light.
I closed the app by accident. Can I get back in?
Reopen the app. If a session is still in progress, it offers to rejoin. Sessions stay open for a short time after the last activity.
Can I see results from a past round?
The most recent session is shown when you reopen the app. We do not store a long history of past rounds on your device.

Account, privacy, and data

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-in. You type a first name when a round starts and that is the only identity used during the game.
How do I delete my data?
Email us with the session code from your results screen and we will delete that session. Sessions older than 90 days may be removed automatically.
